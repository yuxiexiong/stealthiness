- 基础链：Gradient Starvation (NeurIPS 2021, 0987b8b338d6c90bbedd8631bc499221)；Shortcut learning in DNNs (10.1038/s42256-020-00257-z)；Nanda et al. Progress measures (2301.05217)；Grokking as transition lazy/rich (2310.06110)；Mirage (NeurIPS 2023)。
- 直接后门：Backdoor Defense via Suppressing Model Shortcuts (2211.05631)；Backdoor-vaccine: An active backdoor defense framework via competitive backdoor injection (10.1016/j.neucom.2025.132133, 2026 journal)；Backdoor learning curves (10.1007/s13042-024-02363-5)。后两项分别为特征竞争及形成条件接缝；留意同名LLM Backdoor-Vaccine不同论文。

- grokking反证链：Grokking at Edge of Linear Separability (PMLR267 beck25a, 2025)；Grokking at Edge of Numerical Stability (2501.04697)；Post-Grokking Collapse at Representation-Readout Interface (2608.07436)；新norm/spectral理论2603.13331、2609.26679只为次级同类线索，非自动增篇。
