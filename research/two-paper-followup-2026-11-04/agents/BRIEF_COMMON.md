# Common brief (read fully before searching)

## The object being audited
Setting: LoRA fine-tuning of LLaVA-1.5-7B on 20,000 single-word VQA rows, 200 of them poisoned (1%, a FIXED poison set: image-corner trigger -> target word), one epoch = 1,250 optimizer steps, effective batch 16, AdamW, cosine schedule.
Intervention ("skeleton / fill recombination"): hold fixed WHICH poisoned rows are consumed at WHICH training steps (the "poison skeleton"), and permute ONLY the order of the remaining 19,800 clean rows (the "clean fill"). Same multiset of rows, same poison identities and positions, same init, same hyper-parameters.
Readout: the training step at which the backdoor "takes off" (median over 200 triggered probes of [target-token logit minus best competing logit] crosses zero and stays above for 40 steps).
Measured: with the skeleton fixed, swapping the clean fill moves the take-off step by 20-60 steps (e.g. 464 -> 403/410/405; 328 -> 357 / 306), versus <= 5 steps from changing the LoRA-init/dropout seed. An additive fit gives skeleton span ~95 steps and fill span ~53 steps. One particular clean fill delays take-off by 30-60 steps under all three skeletons it was paired with. A companion order x init crossover shows take-off follows the data order, not the init seed. Each cell is one training run.
Template of the claim: "With the exposure schedule of the TARGET examples held fixed (identity + positions), the ORDER of the NON-TARGET / background data alone shifts WHEN a specific target behaviour is acquired, by an amount far above seed noise."
