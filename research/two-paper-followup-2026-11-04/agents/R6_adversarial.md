Object audited: "poison skeleton fixed, clean fill permuted -> backdoor take-off step moves by 20-60 steps (init/dropout seed: <= 5 steps)", LoRA / LLaVA-1.5-7B, 1 epoch = 1,250 steps. Template: "target exposure schedule fixed; order of non-target data alone shifts WHEN the target behaviour is acquired, far above seed noise."

Search intent of this round: find what would make the object already-run, a corollary (with its size), an artefact, or a truism. Nothing here is a verdict on novelty; every row is precedent / constraint / axis difference only.

## 0. Least favourable findings first

1. **A measured fact pointing the other way on the WHEN axis exists, in a different regime.** Singh et al. 2024 (2404.07129, App. I): "We found little to no differences when training seed (responsible for data ordering) was varied", next to "results that differ slightly across init seeds". From-scratch 2-layer transformer, online-sampled ICL task, no number given, readout unspecified. PolyPythias, Aoyama et al., Chen et al. report phase-transition steps that are tight across (joint) seeds. So "data order moves an event time by a lot" is not a regime-independent regularity; any statement of ours has to carry its regime (single-epoch LoRA fine-tune, sparse 1% target, without-replacement pass).
2. **The generic-sensitivity explanation is live and our current control does not exclude it.** Summers & Dinneen 2021 (one-bit weight change gives the same endpoint variability as shuffling), Jordan 2023 App. D ("varying just the first batch of data ... has a similar effect"), 2607.05104 (floating-point reduction order flips 49/300 grok outcomes), 2608.19168 (one replaced row of one batch moves final weights by 44.1% of the seed-to-seed distance). All are endpoint or outcome readouts, none is an event time, and Jordan reports the equivalence FAILS for short training (1-4 epochs) — but none of them measures what a minimal data perturbation does to an event time in a one-epoch LoRA run. Our "<= 5 steps from init/dropout seed" is a perturbation of a different kind (parameters, not data sequence). A minimal-data-perturbation arm (swap two clean rows; replace one clean row) is missing.
3. **Existence of an order effect is a textbook corollary; nobody claims otherwise.** Smith et al. 2021 Eq. 15 context: the O(eps^2) term xi = sum_j sum_{k<j} grad grad C_j grad C_k "is a random variable which depends on the order of the mini-batches"; LeDoux 2026 Sec. 3.1: "It is well known that SGD updates do not commute in non-convex landscapes". What I did NOT find in any text I opened is (a) the size of the effect in event-time units, (b) an experiment that pins the target rows and permutes only the rest.
4. **Fill permutation confounds batch composition with sequence.** LeDoux 2026 Sec. 6.8 states this confound for his own design and leaves it open ("further factorization is left to future work"). Our fill swap changes both which clean rows share a batch with each poison row and what happens between poison batches.
5. **Clean-task progress is a named competing mechanism with theory behind it** (Li & Liu ICML 2024 "race" between trigger and feature vectors; Zhu et al. NeurIPS 2022 "strict order of two stages"; Gu et al. ACL 2023 gradient-direction conflict). If a fill changes how fast the clean VQA task is fitted, the take-off shift could be mediated by clean-loss level at the poison steps rather than by order as such.

What I did not find (after the queries in Sec. 7, 44 full texts): any paper that holds target identities AND positions fixed, permutes only the non-target data, and reads out either an endpoint or a time; any paper reporting backdoor/canary variance across shuffle seeds separately from init seed with the poison set fixed; any source from which the 20-60-step magnitude follows. This is absence in what I searched, not proof of absence (boundaries in Sec. 6).

What C1 demands of the design [derivation from the rows above]: a perturbation-size ladder on the DATA sequence with the skeleton fixed — (i) bit-identical rerun, (ii) swap two clean rows inside one poison-free batch, (iii) swap two whole clean batches, (iv) permute one window, (v) full fill permutation — all read out in take-off steps. If (ii) already gives tens of steps, the effect is generic sensitivity; if displacement grows with the amount permuted and a given fill has a reproducible signed effect across skeletons, it is not. The existing observation "one fill delays take-off under all three skeletons" is the only current evidence of the second kind, and it rests on single runs per cell.
